Magnetism

Magnetism is associated with magnetic fields, which arise from the motion of electric charges. This motion can take many forms.

Core Concepts

Understanding the main topics is key to mastering magnetism.

Magnetic Fields

Evidence for the presence of a magnetic field is the magnetic force on charges moving in that field; the force is at right angles to both the field and the velocity of the charge. This force deflects the particles without changing their speed.

Magnetic fields show the strength and direction of magnetism.

Permanent Magnets

Materials that maintain their magnetic properties for a long time.

Temporary Magnets

Materials, like a nail, that become magnetized in the presence of a permanent magnet but may lose their magnetism.

Electromagnetism

Created by electric currents flowing through a wire, often coiled around an iron core to increase strength.
