Static Electricity

Static electricity is a form of electricity resulting from the imbalance between positive and negative charges within a material that occurs when electrons (the negatively charged particles in an atom) move from one material to another.

Core Concepts

Understanding main topics is key to mastering the concept of static electricity.

Polarization

Polarization in static electricity refers to the separation of electric charges within an object, leading to a distribution of positive and negative charges.

Coulomb's Law

the electrostatic force F between two point charges Q1, Q2 is directly proportional to the product of their magnitudes and inversely proportional to the square of the distance r between them.
